ABOUT AI DOT LABS

Why AI Dot Labs
exists.

Technology is changing faster than classrooms can adapt. AI Dot Labs exists to bridge that gap by bringing practical AI learning into schools—where students can move beyond consuming technology and start creating it.

Our Mission

Educate

Hands-on learning from school to industry.

Certify

Industry-ready mentors and professionals.

Build

Advanced labs with full infrastructure.

Automate

Intelligent solutions for real businesses.

Innovate

Research-grade projects and patents.

Empower

Create creators, not just tech consumers.

Our Vision

To transform every school into a future-ready innovation hub where students learn by building, experimenting, and creating with Artificial Intelligence.

Founder

Mukund Maheshwari

Founder, AI Dot Labs

When I saw how quickly technology was changing, I realized our education system wasn’t keeping up. AI Dot Labs was born from a simple belief — that every student deserves the chance to build, create and shape the future with AI.
